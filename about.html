Jamey Shiels

Fractional CMO and founder
Based in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, working with clients across the US

I grew virtual visits from 10,000 to 850,000 a year in three years, launched online scheduling that brought in $50M in revenue, and took a patient app to 1 million downloads in 18 months.

I've spent my career where marketing, technology and health care meet. At Advocate Health, one of the largest health systems in the country, I led enterprise marketing technology and operations, with a team spanning customer experience, design, development, IT, operations and compliance. Along the way I founded Wellcierge, a digital health company that raised significant investment and was acquired. Today I'm a doctoral researcher studying how organizations adopt AI, and I'm affiliated with Wake Forest's Center for AI Research and Innovation.

I started Exploring Digital because growing firms deserve the same caliber of marketing leadership and systems that large organizations take for granted, without the overhead. I treat marketing as the whole customer experience, from the first ad to the people who answer the phone, because that's what customers remember.

"Marketing has to be fast and careful at the same time. I learned that in health care, and it's the standard I bring to every client."
